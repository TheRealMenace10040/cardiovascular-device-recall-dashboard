4What this means for a quality team

Manufacturing and process issues (37.3%) are the biggest driver, ahead of design (32.8%). That covers process control, nonconforming material, packaging and equipment maintenance. A CAPA program looking at this would start with in-process controls and incoming material inspection before spending more on design verification.

12.2% fall under human error or are still under investigation. Most of that is "Under investigation by firm", where the root cause wasn't final when the recall was reported. It's worth tracking on its own because the final cause could move any of the other categories.

6Data and method
