What this tells a Quality team

Reading the recoded breakdown
Manufacturing/Process issues (37.3%) — process control, nonconforming material, packaging, equipment maintenance — edge out Design issues (32.8%) as the single largest driver of recalls. That's the inverse of what many assume ("recalls are mostly design flaws") and would point a CAPA program toward tightening process controls and incoming material inspection first.
Human/Investigation (12.2%) includes cases still under investigation by the firm at time of report — a leading indicator worth tracking separately, since root cause hasn't been finalized yet.

Data & methodology

  • Source: api.fda.gov/device/recall.json, filtered to openfda.medical_specialty_description:"Cardiovascular", pulled live on Aug 14, 2026.
  • What's real vs. derived: the root-cause counts (top 15) and device-class counts (Class I/II/III) are exact live aggregation results from the API. The four category buckets (Design / Manufacturing / Human / Other) are my own recode of those 15 real labels — a common analyst move to make a long tail of raw categories decision-ready.
  • Known limitation: this build environment hit openFDA's shared-IP rate limit before I could also pull a yearly trend line and a top-manufacturer leaderboard. Both are easy additions — openfda_pull.py (sent alongside this dashboard) is a ready-to-run script that pulls those plus MAUDE adverse-event data; run it locally and drop the output into the DATA block in this file to extend the dashboard.
  • Disclaimer: openFDA data is unvalidated and should not be used for clinical decisions — see FDA terms.
